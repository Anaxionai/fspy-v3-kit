# Exam pack: AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02)

This pack is optional. Nothing in FSPY-V3 depends on it. Use it if you want an AWS certificate after the course.

The pack helps you close the gap between the course and the exam in about three weeks. Plan for 25 to 35 hours of part-time study.

Three points to read before you start:

- **The exam is optional.** You do not need it to finish FSPY-V3.
- **AWS recommends up to six months of exposure to AWS.** The exam guide describes the target candidate this way. The certification page adds that this exposure is not required. FSPY-V3 gave you AWS practice on an emulator and one day on a real account, so plan your study time with care.
- **You pay the exam provider.** You pay Pearson VUE when you book, by card or with a voucher. You do not pay your training centre for the exam.

## Book the exam

1. Ask for extra time first. In your AWS Certification Account, choose Request Exam Accommodations, then "ESL +30 MINUTES". You ask once, before you book, and it applies to every later booking.
2. Open your AWS Certification Account at <https://cp.certmetrics.com/amazon/>. This is the "Schedule an exam" link on <https://aws.amazon.com/certification/>. The name on your account must match the name on your ID exactly.
3. Choose Schedule New Exam, then Schedule at Pearson VUE. Pick a test centre or an online exam, then a date and time.
4. Pay by card (Visa, American Express, Mastercard or JCB) or with a voucher.
5. You can reschedule up to 24 hours before the exam, twice for each booking. If you cancel later than that or miss it, you lose the fee.

The rules are on <https://aws.amazon.com/certification/policies/before-testing/> and <https://aws.amazon.com/certification/policies/during-testing/>.

### What to bring

| You sit it | Bring or prepare |
| --- | --- |
| At a test centre | Two IDs: two primary IDs, or one primary and one secondary. A primary ID is an original, unexpired, government-issued ID. The names must match your AWS Certification Account. Arrive 15 to 30 minutes early. |
| Online | One primary ID. A computer that passes the Pearson VUE system test, run before exam day. You can start up to 30 minutes early. If you are more than 15 minutes late, you lose the fee. |

Online, you get no breaks, and you must stay in view of the camera. Read the online requirements and watch the video at <https://www.pearsonvue.com/us/en/aws/onvue.html>.
