# Optional certification packs

FSPY-V3 does not require a certification. These packs are for students who want one after the course. Each pack maps the exam to what you already did in the course, lists what the course did not teach, and gives a three-week study plan, a 40-question mock exam and its answers.

## Which exam fits, and when

| Exam | Level | Pack | When to start | Study time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02) | Foundational | [`aws-cloud-practitioner/`](aws-cloud-practitioner/) | After P13 (week 22), or after the course | About 25 to 35 hours |
| Microsoft Azure Fundamentals (AZ-900) | Foundational | [`azure-fundamentals/`](azure-fundamentals/) | After the course | About 25 to 35 hours |

Start with AWS. The course works on AWS services on the cloud lab from the map weeks and on a real AWS account in P13, so more of that exam is familiar. The course gives you little Azure practice, so the Azure pack takes more study.

## Why there is no associate pack

Associate exams, such as AWS Certified Developer – Associate or Microsoft's Azure AI Cloud Developer Associate, expect about a year of hands-on work on that cloud. The course runs most cloud work on emulators on your laptop, and real cloud work for one day. You know many of the ideas these exams test, but not the cloud's own services in depth. Aim for one after six to twelve months of real cloud work in a job.

## Fees and discounts

- You pay the exam fee to the exam provider when you book. The course does not pay it.
- AWS lists its foundational exams at USD 100. An INR price (INR 8,553 for a foundational exam, read on 13 November 2026) applies only to vouchers bought on the Pearson VUE MindHub voucher store.
- Microsoft sets its price by country and shows it when you book. Microsoft's student discount does not apply in India.
- After you earn one AWS certification, AWS gives you 50% off other AWS exams. The voucher appears in your AWS Certification Account within five business days.
- If English is not your first language, both AWS and Microsoft let you ask for 30 extra minutes. Ask before you book; each pack says how.
- If you fail an AWS exam, you wait 14 days before you sit it again.

Prices and offers change. Check the official page before you book.

## How to use a pack

1. Read the pack's `README.md` first. Its topic map shows what you know and what you do not.
2. Follow `study-plan.md`, about 10 hours a week.
3. Sit `mock-exam.md` under exam conditions: timed, no notes, no AI. Mark it with `mock-exam-answers.md`.
4. Book the real exam when you score at least 80% on the mock exam.

The exam facts in each pack were read from the official pages on the date the pack states. Exams change: check the official exam page before you book.
