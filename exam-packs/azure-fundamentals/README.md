# Exam pack: Microsoft Azure Fundamentals (AZ-900)

This pack is for FSPY-V3 graduates who want the Microsoft Azure Fundamentals certification. The exam is optional. You can finish the course without it.

FSPY-V3 taught the cloud on AWS, and you never opened an Azure account. Most Azure names and tools on this exam will be new to you. Plan for three weeks of part-time study, 25 to 35 hours in total.

| File | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `README.md` | The exam facts, what you already know, the gap list and how to book |
| `study-plan.md` | A three-week plan in day-sized blocks, with a self-check each week |
| `mock-exam.md` | 40 practice questions written for this pack, with the rules for sitting it |
| `mock-exam-answers.md` | The answers, a reason and an official link for each, and a scoring guide |

We read every exam fact below on Microsoft's official pages on 13 November 2026. Microsoft changes its exams often. Check the certification page and the study guide again before you book.

This pack uses British spelling. It keeps Microsoft's spelling in product names, page titles and exam terms such as "datacenter".

Three things to know before you start:

- **The exam is optional.** FSPY-V3 does not require it. Take it only if you want an Azure line on your CV.
- **You pay the fee to the exam provider.** The provider is Pearson VUE (the company that runs Microsoft's exams). You pay when you book.
- **This pack takes real study.** The course gave you very little Azure practice, so this pack carries most of the Azure learning. Skimming it will not be enough.

### A free Azure account, if you want hands-on practice

You can pass AZ-900 without an Azure account. This pack never requires one. If you open one, follow the safety rules in `study-plan.md`.

On 13 November 2026, the [Azure account page](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/pricing/purchase-options/azure-account) said this:

- You get 200 US dollars of credit to use within 30 days.
- Some services are free for 12 months, and a list of services is always free.
- Sign-up needs a credit or debit card (not prepaid), a phone number and a Microsoft or GitHub account.
- After 30 days you must move to pay-as-you-go to keep using the account. Otherwise Azure disables the account and its services.

The [Azure for Students](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/free/students) offer gives 100 US dollars of credit for 12 months with no card. It is only for full-time university students who sign up with a school email. If you have left university, you probably do not qualify. Neither page shows prices in rupees or mentions India by name.

## How to book the exam

1. Sign in to Microsoft Learn with a personal Microsoft account. Microsoft advises against a work or school account, because you lose its records when you leave.
2. Add your legal name to your Learn profile. It must match your government ID exactly, or you cannot take the exam.
3. If English is not your strong language, ask for extra time first. Use the "English as a second language" request on the [accommodations page](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/available-exam-accommodations-document-requirements). It needs a letter from an instructor or employer.
4. Open the [certification page](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/azure-fundamentals/) and select **Schedule exam** under Pearson VUE. The [booking page](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/schedule-through-pearson-vue?examUid=exam.AZ-900&examUrl=https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications) asks for your address, phone number and email.
5. Choose an online exam or a test centre. For an online exam, run the system test first on the same computer, in the same room.
6. Pay the fee shown at booking. You can book up to 90 days ahead, and hold at most two Microsoft exam bookings at a time.

The full steps are on [Register and schedule an exam](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/register-schedule-exam). Students at an academic institution may see a second option, Certiport. Most graduates book through Pearson VUE.

### What to bring and prepare

- **ID:** a current government-issued ID. The name must match your Learn profile exactly.
- **Online exam:** a walled room with the door closed, and nobody else in it. Clear the desk of books, notes, paper and pens.
- **Online exam:** a mobile phone for the check-in photos of your face, ID and desk. Then place it out of arm's reach.
- **Online exam:** a personal computer where you have administrator rights. Work laptops often block the exam software.
- **Online exam:** check in from 30 minutes before your start time. You can still check in up to 15 minutes after it.
- **Test centre:** your ID, and the arrival time from your Pearson VUE booking email.
- **Rules for both:** no notes, no other person and no AI tool may help you.

Sources: [About online exams with Pearson VUE](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/online-exams) and [Pearson VUE for Microsoft](https://home.pearsonvue.com/microsoft).
