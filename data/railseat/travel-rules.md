# RailSeat travel rules

> **RailSeat is a training product for the FSPY-V3 course.**
> These rules are made up for practice. They are not the rules of Indian Railways or IRCTC.
> Never use them to plan a real journey.

Version 1, November 2026. There are 40 rules. Each rule has a number.
When you answer a question from these rules, cite the rule number, for example **[Rule 26]**.

Words used in the rules:

- **Fare:** the price of one passenger's ticket, before any fee.
- **Base fare:** the fare before a discount or a last-minute charge.
- **Departure:** the time the train is due to leave your boarding station, as printed on your ticket.
- **Chart:** the final list of passengers and berths for a journey (see Rule 21).
- **PNR:** the booking's 10-character code of capital letters and digits, such as `K7Q2M9X4P1`.

## Booking

### Rule 1: When booking opens
Booking for a journey opens 90 days before the date of travel, at 08:00. It closes when the chart is prepared (Rule 21).

### Rule 2: Passengers in one booking
One booking (one PNR) holds 1 to 6 passengers. To book for 7 or more people, make two or more bookings.

### Rule 3: The lead passenger
Every booking names a lead passenger. The lead passenger must be 18 or older and must travel on the journey.

### Rule 4: How to pay
Pay by UPI, debit card, credit card or net banking. RailSeat does not take cash. A booking is confirmed only after the payment succeeds.

### Rule 5: Berth preference
You may ask for a lower, middle, upper, side lower or side upper berth. RailSeat tries to give you that berth, but it does not promise it.

### Rule 6: Changing a passenger's name
You may change a passenger's name once, up to 24 hours before departure. The new passenger must be a parent, spouse, child, brother or sister of the old one. The fee is ₹50 per passenger.

### Rule 7: Changing the boarding station
You may change your boarding station once, up to 24 hours before departure, to a later station on the same route. RailSeat does not refund the part of the route you skip.

## Fares and discounts

### Rule 8: Children under 4
Children aged 0 to 3 travel free if they share an adult's berth or seat. Each adult may bring at most two such children. Add them to the booking with their age.

### Rule 9: Children aged 4 to 11
Children aged 4 to 11 pay 60% of the adult fare. They always get their own berth or seat.

### Rule 10: Passengers aged 12 and over
Passengers aged 12 and over pay the full adult fare.

### Rule 11: Senior citizens
Passengers aged 60 or older get 30% off the base fare in SL, 3A and CC. There is no senior citizen discount in 1A or 2A.

### Rule 12: Students
Full-time students aged 18 to 25 get 15% off the base fare in SL and CC on journeys of 300 km or more. Carry your college identity card on the journey.

### Rule 13: A companion of a passenger with a disability
One companion travelling with a passenger who has a certified disability pays 50% of the fare, in any class.

### Rule 14: One discount for each passenger
Each passenger gets at most one discount: the largest one they qualify for. No discount applies to the last-minute quota (Rule 20).

## Luggage

### Rule 15: Free luggage
Each adult passenger may carry this much luggage free: 1A 60 kg, 2A 45 kg, 3A 35 kg, SL 30 kg, CC 20 kg. A child aged 4 to 11 may carry half of the adult amount.

### Rule 16: Extra luggage
You may carry up to 30 kg more than your free amount. Pay ₹15 for each extra kilogram for every 500 km of the journey, or part of 500 km. Pay at the luggage counter before the train leaves.

### Rule 17: Size of bags
No single bag may be larger than 100 cm × 60 cm × 25 cm. Bicycles, two-wheelers and other large items travel in the luggage van, booked separately at the station.

### Rule 18: Items that are never allowed
Gas cylinders, petrol, kerosene, fireworks, acids and other dangerous goods are never allowed on a RailSeat train, in any amount.

### Rule 19: Lost luggage
Report lost luggage to the train staff and in the RailSeat app within 24 hours. RailSeat is responsible only for luggage booked in the luggage van.

## Last-minute quota

### Rule 20: Last-minute quota
A few berths of each train open one day before departure: at 10:00 for 1A, 2A and 3A, and at 11:00 for SL and CC. They cost 30% more than the base fare. Last-minute tickets get no refund when you cancel them.

## The chart and the waitlist

### Rule 21: Chart preparation
The chart is prepared 6 hours before the train leaves its first station. After the chart is prepared, you cannot change a booking.

### Rule 22: Joining the waitlist
When a class is full, you may join its waitlist. Each class on each journey has at most 30 waitlist places. Your ticket shows your place, for example WL 7.

### Rule 23: Moving up the waitlist
When a passenger with a confirmed berth cancels, the first person on the waitlist gets that berth automatically. Everyone else on the waitlist moves up one place.

### Rule 24: Still waitlisted when the chart is prepared
If a ticket is still waitlisted when the chart is prepared, RailSeat cancels it automatically. You get the full fare back, minus ₹25 per passenger. A waitlisted passenger may not board the train.

### Rule 25: Part of a booking confirmed, part waitlisted
A PNR may have confirmed passengers and waitlisted passengers when the chart is prepared. Then the confirmed passengers may travel. The waitlisted passengers get their money back under Rule 24.

## Cancellation and refunds

### Rule 26: Cancelling more than 72 hours before departure
The cancellation fee is a fixed amount per passenger: 1A ₹300, 2A ₹250, 3A ₹200, CC ₹150 and SL ₹100. You get the rest of the fare back.

### Rule 27: Cancelling 72 to 24 hours before departure
The cancellation fee is 20% of the fare per passenger. It is never less than the fixed fee for the class in Rule 26.

### Rule 28: Cancelling 24 to 6 hours before departure
The cancellation fee is 40% of the fare per passenger.

### Rule 29: Cancelling less than 6 hours before departure
If you cancel less than 6 hours before departure, or after the chart is prepared, you get no refund. Rules 30, 31 and 32 are the only exceptions.

### Rule 30: The train is cancelled
If RailSeat cancels the train, you get the full fare back automatically within 3 working days. You do not need to cancel the ticket yourself.

### Rule 31: The train is more than 3 hours late
The train may leave your boarding station more than 3 hours late. If you then decide not to travel, cancel the ticket before the train leaves your boarding station. You get the full fare back.

### Rule 32: The air conditioning fails
If the air conditioning in your coach fails for more than 2 hours of the journey, you get money back. You get the difference between your fare and the SL fare. Ask for it in the RailSeat app within 48 hours after the train arrives.

### Rule 33: How refunds are paid
A refund goes back to the way you paid. A UPI refund arrives within 2 working days. A card or net banking refund arrives within 7 working days.

## Identity and travel

### Rule 34: Identity proof
Every passenger aged 18 or older must carry an original photo identity card: an Aadhaar card, passport, driving licence, voter ID card or PAN card. A copy in DigiLocker on your phone is accepted.

### Rule 35: Travelling without identity proof
A passenger who cannot show identity proof is treated as travelling without a ticket. That passenger pays a fine of ₹500 plus the fare.

### Rule 36: Missing the train
If you miss your train, you get no refund, except under Rule 31. You cannot use the ticket on another train.

### Rule 37: Smoking and alcohol
Smoking and drinking alcohol are not allowed on RailSeat trains or on the platforms. The fine is ₹500.

## Pets

### Rule 38: Which pets may travel
Only dogs and cats may travel. They travel only in 1A, and only when your group books the whole cabin of 2 or 4 berths.

### Rule 39: Pet fee and papers
The pet fee is ₹750 per pet per journey, paid when you book. Carry the pet's vaccination certificate, dated within the last 12 months.

### Rule 40: Guide dogs
A guide dog travelling with a passenger who has a visual disability travels free, in any class. Rule 38 does not apply to guide dogs.
